Agriculture began at different times in different parts of the world. People cultivated plants and managed animals while many communities continued to rely on hunting and gathering.

Where food production took hold, it supported more permanent settlements and new patterns of work. The crops, animals, and pace of change varied by region; there was no single route into farming.
