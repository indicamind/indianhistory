## About this edict

This short pillar inscription records two acts at different regnal dates: enlargement of the stupa and the king’s later visit. The facsimile shares a plate with the Lumbini inscription.

## Roman transliteration

The text below follows the Nigali Sagar version in Hultzsch’s edition. Square brackets show restored or uncertain letters; dots mark damaged or missing text. Other surviving copies can differ.

## English translation

This is E. Hultzsch’s 1925 translation of the same version. Its terminology and bracketed restorations reflect that historical edition.

> (A) When king Devānāṃpriya Priyadarśin had been anointed fourteen years, he enlarged the Stūpa of the Buddha Konākamana to the double (of its original size).
> (B) And when he had been anointed [twenty] years, he came himself and worshipped (this spot) [and] caused [a stone pillar to be set up].
