## About this inscription

This short dedication names King Priyadarśin, conventionally identified with Ashoka, and grants the Banyan Cave at Barabar to the Ājīvikas. The text dates the gift to the twelfth year after his consecration. The facsimile plate also includes the other two Barabar cave inscriptions; the Sudama text is the first of the three.

## Roman transliteration

The text below follows Hultzsch’s edition of the Sudama inscription. Square brackets mark restored or uncertain letters.

<p class="inscription-transliteration" lang="pra-Latn">1 lājinā Piyadasinā duvāḍasa-[vasābhisitenā] 2 [iyaṃ Nigoha]-kubhā di[nā ājīvikehi]</p>

## English translation

This is the complete surviving text in E. Hultzsch’s 1925 English translation. The brackets reflect damaged passages in his edition.

> By king Priyadarśin, (when he had been) anointed twelve years, this Banyan-cave was given to the Ājīvikas.
