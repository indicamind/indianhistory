## About this inscription

This second Barabar dedication records another cave granted to the Ājīvikas in the twelfth year after King Priyadarśin’s consecration. The inscription names the Khalatika mountain but does not give the cave’s modern name. It is the second text on the shared facsimile plate.

## Roman transliteration

The text below follows Hultzsch’s edition of the Visvakarma inscription. Square brackets mark restored or uncertain letters.

<p class="inscription-transliteration" lang="pra-Latn">1 lājinā Piyadasinā duvā2ḍasa-vasābhisitenā iyaṃ 3 kubhā Khalatika-pavatasi 4 dinā [ājīvi]kehi</p>

## English translation

This is the complete surviving text in E. Hultzsch’s 1925 English translation. The brackets reflect damaged passages in his edition.

> By king Priyadarśin, (when he had been) anointed twelve years, this cave in the Khalatika mountain was given to the Ājīvikas.
