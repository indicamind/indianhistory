## More than one way to farm

Early farming communities did not all live in the same way. Some grew crops and cared for animals while still hunting, fishing, or gathering wild foods. Local plants, animals, and environments shaped the choices available to each community.

## Traces of daily life

Archaeologists study seeds, animal bones, tools, and remains of buildings to understand how people produced food and lived together. The evidence differs from one site to another, so the history of farming is reconstructed region by region.
