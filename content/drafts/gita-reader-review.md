## Reading

Each card presents Meaning, Explanation, Sanskrit and IAST in that order. Side arrows, swipes, keyboard controls, direct verse selection and search scroll instantly to a verse. Navigation continues across chapters and wraps the book. Reading progress is restored on reload. On mobile, the compact chapter/verse toolbar joins the site header.

## Publication and languages

The approved revised English is available in normal web and offline readers, without a draft or preview notice. At the user’s request, 77 existing non-English Gita commentary translation files were deleted. Other site content and interface translations remain intact. Localized Gita views retain the original Sanskrit and IAST, without English commentary fallback. Restart Gita commentary translation only on a new request.

These inline chapter sources remain excluded from ordinary article lists; no article memberships or ancestor links changed. Source notes and external references remain in each chapter’s final Sources. The all-language gate remains available for future requested translations while approved English is released independently.

## Validation
